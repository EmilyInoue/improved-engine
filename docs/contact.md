### Frequently Asked Questions

#### How long does it take to get a response?

We typically respond to inquiries within 24-48 hours during business days.

#### Can I contribute to the blog?

Absolutely! We welcome guest posts and contributions. Please reach out through the contact form above with your ideas.

#### Is this site open source?

Yes! The entire site is available on GitHub. Feel free to fork it, submit pull requests, or use it as a template for your own projects.

#### How was this site built?

This site is built using:

- **MkDocs**: Static site generator
- **Material for MkDocs**: Beautiful, responsive theme
- **Python**: Backend technology
- **Markdown**: Content format
- **GitHub Pages**: Hosting platform

Check out our [blog post on getting started with MkDocs](blog/posts/getting-started-mkdocs.md) to learn more!
